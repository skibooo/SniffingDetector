Prevention Recommendations

ARP Poisoning / Flooding

  • Configure static ARP entries for critical devices such as the gateway.
  • Enable Dynamic ARP Inspection (DAI) on managed switches and routers.
  • Isolate or disconnect the device sending suspicious ARP replies.

Suspicious DNS Lookups

  • Use trusted DNS servers and enable DNS over HTTPS (DoH) or DNS over TLS.
  • Investigate the host performing mass reverse DNS lookups.
  • Block unauthorised DNS traffic at the firewall.

Promiscuous Mode Detected

  • Locate and inspect the flagged device for sniffing software.
  • Segment the network with VLANs to limit what a sniffer can see.
  • Remove unauthorised devices from the network.

General Best Practices

  • Use WPA3 (or WPA2-AES) with a strong passphrase on the wireless network.
  • Encrypt traffic end to end with HTTPS, SSH and VPN.
  • Keep router firmware updated and disable WPS.
